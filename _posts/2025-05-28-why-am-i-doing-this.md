# Why do I have this blog?
#### Because I am a nerd.


I thought it might be good to clear up why exactly I even have a blog.

I often find myself getting caught up in tech, math, or otherwise nerdy rabbit holes. In that process, I end up doing and learning a lot of cool stuff, and I wanted somewhere to pool all of that knowledge and *stuff* into one place.

None of this is assigned work, or copied from somewhere else, just breadcrumbs of my journeys in what I find interesting.
